Codec notes

Pick the compression goal first

Lossless

PNG keeps decoded pixels exact and usually helps graphics, screenshots, and art with sharp edges.

Lossy

JPEG, WebP, and AVIF trade imperceptible detail for much smaller photo files.

Palette

PNG palette compression reduces the color set, which is useful for icons, diagrams, and flat artwork.

Standards edge

JPEG XL and neural codecs are important, but normal browsers do not expose reliable client-side encoders yet.
